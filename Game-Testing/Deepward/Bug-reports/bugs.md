# Deepward — Bug Report Summary

Three candidate defects were identified during the exploratory session and converted into structured bug reports.

| ID | Title | Component | Severity | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [BUG-DW-001](BUG-DW-001-first-kick-no-stamina-cost.md) | First standing kick does not consume stamina | Combat / Stamina | Major | High | New |
| [BUG-DW-002](BUG-DW-002-kick-input-drains-extra-stamina.md) | Repeated kick input consumes stamina without matching kick actions | Combat / Input | Major | High | New |
| [BUG-DW-003](BUG-DW-003-potion-consumed-at-full-health.md) | Healing potion is consumed while the PC is at full HP | Healing / Inventory | Major | Medium | New |

## Classification Rationale

All three issues affect gameplay resources or combat rules but do not prevent continued play, so they are classified as **Major** rather than Critical. Priority reflects the likely effect on combat balance and player progress.

## Evidence Note

The behaviors were recorded during exploratory testing and included in the structured requirements source. No dedicated reproduction screenshots or videos were captured. Each report states this limitation explicitly; evidence has not been invented or reconstructed.
